About the orchard.

An original, small arcade adventure about collecting light in a busy universe.

One simple idea

Orbit Orchard turns a falling-star sky into a short reflex challenge. You pilot a small collecting vessel, gather golden starlight, and weave around coral space debris. The name imagines the night sky as an orchard: a place to gather something bright.

Designed for a quick break

Sessions begin with three lives. Every ten collected stars advances the level, gradually increasing the pace. There is no final level: the goal is to improve your own score. Missed stars carry no penalty, so you can choose safety over a risky catch.

Built for the browser

The game uses original canvas graphics and synthesized sound, with no external assets or libraries. It supports keyboard, mouse, and touch input. Sound is optional, and your personal leaderboard is stored locally. No account is required.
