Privacy policy.

The game works without an account. Your scores stay on your device.

Last updated: October 8, 2026.

Local game storage

Orbit Orchard stores up to five high scores and your sound preference in your browser’s local storage under keys beginning with “orbit-orchard”. These records are not sent to us. You can clear scores with the button on the game page. To remove all preferences, delete this site’s data in your browser settings. If storage is unavailable, the game still works, but settings and scores may not persist.

Information collected by this website

The game has no account system or contact form and does not request your name, email address, or precise location. This website includes Google AdSense advertising code, which makes requests to Google and may process information as described below. There is no separate analytics integration. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure the website. Those practices depend on the provider chosen by the site operator.

External links

The contact page links to GitHub. Following that link takes you to a separate service governed by its own privacy policy. Information posted to a public issue may be visible to others.

Advertising

This website uses Google AdSense. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Advertising requests may include your IP address, browser and device information, and identifiers. Google’s advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve personalized ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet, subject to applicable consent choices. Ad availability depends on Google’s approval and account settings.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy for its data handling and retention practices. You can manage personalized advertising in Google My Ad Center and learn about other vendors’ opt-out options at AboutAds. Browser settings can also block or remove cookies; disabling personalization does not necessarily stop all ads. Where required, the site operator must configure a consent mechanism before serving ads. This website does not yet include a consent-management interface.

Children and personal information

The game does not ask players to submit personal information. Please avoid posting personal details in public feedback. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Questions and changes

For general privacy questions, use the contact page. Any future changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
