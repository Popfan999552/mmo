YOUR SPACE, YOUR DATA

Privacy policy.

The game works without an account. Your scores stay on your device.

Last updated: October 8, 2026.

Local game storage

Orbit Orchard stores up to five high scores and your sound preference in your browser’s local storage under keys beginning with “orbit-orchard”. These records are not sent to us. You can clear scores with the button on the game page. To remove all preferences, delete this site’s data in your browser settings. If storage is unavailable, the game still works, but settings and scores may not persist.

Information collected by this website

This version contains no analytics, advertising code, tracking pixels, account system, or contact form. It does not request your name, email address, or precise location. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure the website. Those practices depend on the provider chosen by the site operator.

External links

The contact page links to GitHub. Following that link takes you to a separate service governed by its own privacy policy. Information posted to a public issue may be visible to others.

Advertising

No advertisements are currently served. If advertising is introduced, this policy must be updated to identify the provider, describe its cookies and data use, and explain applicable consent and opt-out choices before those technologies are enabled.

Children and personal information

The game does not ask players to submit personal information. Please avoid posting personal details in public feedback. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Questions and changes

For general privacy questions, use the contact page. Any future changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
