YOUR FIELD GUIDE

Find your rhythm.

Catch golden stars. Avoid coral debris. Keep the orchard glowing.

1. Start your adventure

Choose “Start exploring” on the game screen. You begin at level one with three lives and zero points.

2. Move your vessel

On a keyboard, hold the left and right arrows or A and D. With a mouse, move the pointer inside the game. On a phone or tablet, touch and drag across the sky. Your vessel stays near the bottom of the screen.

3. Collect and progress

Each golden star gives you 100 points. Every ten collected stars starts a new level. Objects fall faster and appear more often as you progress. Let a star fall past you if catching it would put you in danger.

4. Protect your three lives

Touching coral debris costs one life. Your vessel briefly glows after a hit, giving you a moment of protection. At zero lives, the run ends and your score joins the local leaderboard if it is one of your five best.

Take a breath

Press P or the Pause button to pause. Switching tabs also pauses the game. Restart begins a fresh run; unfinished runs are not saved. Sound can be toggled at any time. Scores remain on this browser until you clear them or delete browser data.
